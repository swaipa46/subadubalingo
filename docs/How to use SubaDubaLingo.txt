SUBADUBALINGO - subtitles and dubbing for videos
================================================
(The program was called Videosprog until version 2.0.)

The program makes subtitles for videos and can dub them into Swedish, Norwegian,
Danish or English with a computer voice. It can also cut out waiting time,
pauses and things that shouldn't be in, for example from a Teams recording.
Everything happens on your own PC. The video isn't sent anywhere.

Start the program by double-clicking "SubaDubaLingo.exe" in the folder.
If you already have Videosprog, it offers to update: press "Update". Your
settings and words are kept, and the shortcuts are moved. If Claude is
open, close Claude completely afterwards and open it again.
The first time, the program offers to install itself; press "Install".
Tip: right-click it and choose "Pin to Start" so it's easy to find.

At the top right you can change the program's language (DA, SV, NO, EN) and look
(Light, Night or Follow Windows). The cog wheel at the far right opens the
settings.


THE FIRST TIME
--------------
The first time you press Start, the program says some files are missing
(about 1.1 GB, plus 63 MB per voice for dubbing). Press "Download automatically".
If that doesn't work, click the links, download the files and put them in the model
folder (the "Open model folder" button).

Also add the Claude skill to Claude (once). The program shows the
"Add the Claude skill" window by itself, with four steps:
1. Click "Save skill file". It goes into your Downloads folder.
2. Open Claude and go to Customize -> Skills.
3. Delete the old "subadubalingo" and "videosprog" skills if they are there.
4. Click "Upload skill" and choose "subadubalingo-skill.zip".
Then click "I've done it". The skill teaches Claude the rules and EG's product
terms (such as Membercare).


HOW TO MAKE A VIDEO
-------------------
1. Click "Choose video..." and pick the video.
2. Choose the language spoken in the video.
3. Choose what the new video should have:
     Speech:        Original, or a language to dub it into
     Subtitles:     None, or the language the subtitles should be in
   Below your choices it says which file you will get.
4. Press Start. You can get on with other work in the meantime.

While the program works, it shows the steps and how far it has got.
When the text is written, the program comes to the front, and the "Claude" panel
on the right lights up with "Your turn" (see CLAUDE below). After that the program
does the rest by itself, and when the video is ready it says "Done!" with buttons
to play it, show it in the folder or make a new one.

Examples:
  Speech: Original,  Subtitles: Danish     -> the video with Danish subtitles
  Speech: Original,  Subtitles: Swedish    -> the video with Swedish subtitles
  Speech: Swedish,   Subtitles: Norwegian  -> Swedish dub with Norwegian subtitles

The new files go in the same folder as the video. The original is never changed.
The text is saved, so the next video from the same recording (say, another language)
is much faster. If the video has changed since, the text is redone automatically.

It takes time: on an ordinary laptop it can take a couple of minutes per minute
of video. The program shows how much time is left and lets other programs
go first, so you can keep working. If the PC still gets too
slow (in a Teams meeting, say), press "Pause". The program then stops using
the PC completely, and when you press "Resume" it carries on where it
left off. Don't close the program while it's working - it would have to start over.
While it's your turn (Claude or the cuts), "Pause" is greyed out: the program
is just waiting for you.

"Cancel" asks first whether you want to stop. Afterwards it tells you whether anything
was saved (such as the Danish subtitles, so next time is faster).


CLAUDE
------
Claude fixes misheard words (product names, for example) and translates into the
languages the new video needs. It works with one file there and one file back:
1. Click "Copy file" in the Claude card.
2. Open a new chat in Claude, paste (Ctrl+V) and send. (If you can't paste
   the file, click "Show in folder" and drag the file into Claude.)
3. Claude makes a reply file ("... - fra Claude.txt"). Click it in Claude's reply
   and press Download.
4. The program finds the file in your Downloads folder by itself, checks it and
   carries on. There's nothing to save or move. Meanwhile, step 3 shows
   that the program is waiting for the reply file, and for how long.

If the reply file isn't right (for example if Claude didn't finish), it tells you what
to do: click "Copy message for Claude", paste the message into the same chat, and
download the new file. (The technical reason is in small grey text below.) If it keeps acting up, you can use "Use copy/paste in parts instead".

The program remembers the words Claude corrects, so they come out right next time.
Every now and then you can give Claude the new words: the cog wheel at the top right ->
"Save Claude skill...", then upload the saved file to Claude as described under
THE FIRST TIME (delete the old skill first).


FIXING MISTAKES IN THE TEXT
---------------------------
The computer doesn't always hear correctly, especially names and technical terms.

If it gets the same word wrong every time (a product name, for example), add it to
the list of corrections: the cog wheel -> "Edit corrections". One line per word,
for example:   member care = Membercare
Then tick "Listen through the video again" and press Start.

You can fix the odd mistake directly in the text file (for example "... - Danish subtitles.srt")
in Notepad. Only change the text, not the numbers and timecodes. Then press Start again.

If you're in a hurry, you can choose "Fastest" under the cog wheel. It's about 1.5
times faster, but gets a few more words wrong.


CUT THE VIDEO FIRST (for example a Teams recording)
---------------------------------------------------
Tick "Cut the video first" before you press Start. The program then
finds long pauses by itself, and Claude suggests what to cut out:
the waiting time before the meeting gets going, interruptions, the end where the recording
drags on, and anything inappropriate, off-topic or impossible to make out.

When Claude is done, the program shows a timeline with all the cuts in colour.
Nothing is cut until you have approved it:
- Cut 1 is selected to begin with. Click a cut to see the frame just before
  and just after it, and press
  "Watch the transition" to see how the cut will look.
- Drag the edges to move a cut. Drag on an empty spot to make
  your own cut. Double-click a cut (or press "Keep in the video") if
  it should stay in the video after all.
- Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in (Shift + mouse wheel scrolls sideways).
- In the list: Space keeps a cut or cuts it out again, and Delete
  removes it.
Press "Cut the video" when you're happy. You get "... - cut.mp4", and
the subtitles and dub are made for the cut video. If you just want
the video cut, choose Speech: Original and Subtitles: None.

If you later make another language of the same video, the cut video is reused.
To cut it again, tick "Cut the video again".


USE THE PROGRAM FROM CLAUDE (optional)
--------------------------------------
You can also ask Claude to do the whole thing in an ordinary chat:
1. The cog wheel -> "Connect to Claude" (once). Close Claude completely
   (right-click the Claude icon by the clock -> Quit) and open Claude again.
2. Write something like this to Claude:
     Make this video in Swedish with Norwegian subtitles:
     C:\Users\you\Videos\webinar.mp4
Claude starts the program, tells you how far it has got, corrects and translates
the text itself and lets you know when the video is ready. You can follow along in
the program meanwhile. If Claude stops along the way, write "continue".
If the video should be cut, say so, for example "Cut out the waiting time and the pauses and
make English subtitles". When you see the cuts in the program, you can change them
there or tell Claude, for example "keep the one at 14:20".
To stop a video, write "stop the video" to Claude.
If you updated the program while Claude was open, Claude will tell you. Then
close Claude completely (Quit, as in step 1) and open Claude again.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Under the cog wheel at the top right there's an "Open log file" button. Send the file to
Kasper.
